## What we collect

One piece of data: the email address you enter in the beta sign-up form, with the date of
your sign-up and of its confirmation.

We set no cookies and use no advertising trackers. Audience measurement, when active, goes
through Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies or persistent
identifiers.

## Why

For one purpose only: to tell you when the Occulis beta opens and send you what you need to
take part. The legal basis is your consent, given by ticking the box on the form and then
confirming your address through the link you receive.

## Where and for how long

Addresses are stored in the publisher's database, hosted by Cloudflare. They are not sold,
rented or shared. Messages are sent through the Resend email service, which receives the
address only at the time of sending.

An unconfirmed sign-up is deleted after seven days. A confirmed sign-up is kept until the
end of the beta, or until you unsubscribe.
